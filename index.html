About

I am an assistant professor in the Mathematics of Operations Research group at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science of the University of Twente, and a member of the Digital Society Institute. My work sits in algorithmic game theory, between theoretical computer science, combinatorial optimisation and economics.

I received my PhD from RWTH Aachen University in 2010, advised by Berthold Vöcking, with a dissertation on the complexity of equilibria in games with succinct representation. Before joining Twente in 2018, I was a junior professor at Paderborn University, where I led a research group at the Heinz Nixdorf Institute and a subproject on markets for services in a DFG Collaborative Research Centre.

In Twente my research has increasingly turned to applications in energy systems, for example how charging decisions of batteries and electric vehicles can be coordinated through prices and incentives.
